PLACEHOLDER FOR OMINOUS SOUND

To activate the creepy hover sound:
1. Download an ominous sound effect (MP3 format)
2. Name it: ominous-hover.mp3
3. Place it in this directory
4. The sound will automatically play on hover

Suggested sound sources:
- https://freesound.org (search: "horror ambient", "metal creak")
- https://www.zapsplat.com (Horror category)
- https://soundbible.com (Scary sounds)

Sound characteristics:
- Duration: 1-2 seconds
- Type: Low rumble or metal groan
- Volume: Subtle (0.3 volume in code)
